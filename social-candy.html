Social Candy Athletics

In my opinion, Social Candy Athletics is the best gym in Milwaukee for bodybuilding. It is a smaller, private facility, so it never feels crowded, and almost everyone there is serious about lifting. The equipment is top quality, and there are plenty of machines for hitting every muscle group.

What I like most is the atmosphere. When everyone around you is training hard, it pushes you to train harder too. It costs more than a basic gym, but if your goal is to build muscle, I think it is worth it.

My Top Reasons to Train Here (Ranked)

  1. High-quality strength equipment built for bodybuilding
  2. A serious, motivating lifting atmosphere
  3. Never too crowded, so you are not waiting for machines
  4. Open around the clock for members
