East Bank Athletic & Social

I think East Bank Athletic & Social is the best gym in Milwaukee for the price. It is located downtown on Water Street in the building that used to be Gold's Gym, and it has five floors of space. For what you pay each month, you get a lot more than you would at most other gyms.

Besides the usual weights and cardio machines, East Bank has extras like a pool, a basketball court, and group classes. It also has a social feel, which makes it a fun place to work out with friends. If you want a little bit of everything without paying a lot, this is the gym I would recommend.

What You Get

  • Strength and cardio equipment
  • A swimming pool
  • A basketball court
  • Group fitness classes
  • A friendly, social atmosphere
