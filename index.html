Where to Lift in Milwaukee

Milwaukee has a lot of great places to work out, but not every gym is right for every person. I have trained at a few different spots around the city, and these are the three I think are worth checking out. Social Candy Athletics is my pick for serious bodybuilding, East Bank Athletic & Social gives you the most for your money, and the Wisconsin Athletic Club has the most amenities.
