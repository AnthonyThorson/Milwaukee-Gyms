Wisconsin Athletic Club

The Wisconsin Athletic Club, or "the WAC," is a full-service club with several locations around the Milwaukee area. One membership lets you use any of their clubs, which is really convenient if you work in one part of town and live in another.

In my opinion, the WAC has the most amenities of any gym on this list. It is more expensive than the other two, but you are paying for a lot of extras. It is a great choice for someone who wants more than just a weight room, or for a family that wants a place everyone can use.

Amenities

Multiple Locations
Clubs all around the Milwaukee area, all included with one membership.
Group Classes
Yoga, cycling, pilates, Zumba, and more.
Pools
Open swim, family swim, and water fitness classes.
Personal Training
Trainers who can help you build a workout plan.
